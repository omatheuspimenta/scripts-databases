Supplementary Figure 3. Processing outcome of the compendium.
Number of Saccharum RNA-seq runs at each stage of the workflow. Of the 3,372
paired-end Illumina runs retrieved from the Sequence Read Archive across 158
BioProjects, 3,365 runs from 156 BioProjects were successfully quantified and 7
were excluded: 3 runs retained fewer than 10,000 reads after trimming and were
skipped by the workflow's minimum-trimmed-read threshold, and 4 runs yielded no
fragments assignable to the R570 reference index. Both affected BioProjects
lost all of their runs. Of the quantified runs, 3,097 (92.0%) passed all post-hoc quality
criteria and 268 (8.0%) were flagged by at least one criterion. Exclusion and
flagging criteria are defined in Supplementary Tables 2-4.


Supplementary Figure 4. Distribution of library size.
Kernel density of the number of raw reads per run (both mates, log10 scale)
across the 3,365 quantified runs. Median 51,654,720 reads per run
(interquartile range 32.1-68.7 million; range 45,425-610,232,490).


Supplementary Figure 5. Reads retained after adapter and quality trimming.
Histogram of the percentage of reads surviving Trim Galore trimming, in 0.1%
bins. Median retention 99.82% (interquartile range 99.35-99.95%). The
horizontal axis is restricted to 95-100% for legibility; 92 runs (2.7%)
retained less than 95% of their reads and fall outside the plotted range, the
lowest being 47.5%.


Supplementary Figure 6. Adapter content of raw reads.
Histogram of the percentage of reads containing adapter sequence prior to
trimming, as reported by Cutadapt. Median 33.2% (interquartile range
31.0-35.3%).


Supplementary Figure 7. FastQC module verdicts before and after trimming.
Share of runs receiving a pass, warn or fail verdict for each FastQC module,
shown separately for raw reads (left) and reads after trimming (right). Modules
are ordered by their failure rate in raw reads. Percentages are printed on
every segment so that the verdict is conveyed by label as well as by colour.
Trimming removed adapter contamination effectively: runs failing the
adapter-content module fell from 359 to 38, and per-base sequence quality
failures from 65 to 13. The high failure rates that persist for per-base
sequence content and per-sequence GC content are expected for plant
transcriptomes, reflecting the non-uniform base composition of random-primed
RNA-seq libraries and the multimodal GC distribution of the highly polyploid
Saccharum transcriptome, rather than technical failure. Counts underlying this
figure are given in Supplementary Table 6.


Supplementary Figure 8. Mapping rate against the R570 transcriptome.
Histogram of the percentage of fragments assigned by Salmon to the R570
reference transcriptome, per run. Median 77.2% (interquartile range
60.7-82.9%); 3,182 runs (94.6%) mapped at or above the 30% quality-flagging
threshold and 2,846 (84.6%) mapped at 50% or above.


Supplementary Figure 9. Gene detection against sequencing depth.
Number of genes detected at TPM > 0 per run, plotted against the number of
fragments mapped by Salmon (log10 scale). Each point is one run, coloured by
its post-hoc quality verdict. Median 111,026 of 190,973 genes detected per run
(interquartile range 99,474-120,674). Flagged runs lie almost entirely in the
low-depth region of the distribution.


Supplementary Figure 10. Mapping rate by BioProject.
Median mapping rate for each of the 156 BioProjects contributing quantified
runs, ordered along the horizontal axis by that median. Vertical bars span the
interquartile range of runs within each BioProject; the 36 BioProjects
contributing a single run have no bar. Seven BioProjects have a median mapping
rate below the 30% quality-flagging threshold.


Supplementary Figure 11. Library type inferred by Salmon.
Number of runs assigned to each Salmon library type. I, inward-facing paired
reads; S, single-end; F and R denote the strand of the first read; U denotes an
unstranded protocol. Salmon inferred a stranded protocol for 1,757 runs (52.2%)
and an unstranded protocol for 1,608 runs (47.8%). Strandedness is not declared
in SRA metadata and is inferred here from the data. The four runs typed SF or
SR were delivered as a single FASTQ file and quantified as single-end despite
being recorded as PAIRED in SRA; they are retained and flagged. Full
library-type concordance between SRA metadata and Salmon inference is reported
in Supplementary Tables 9-11.
